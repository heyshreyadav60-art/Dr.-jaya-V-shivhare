Common Questions

What patients ask

Is homoeopathy safe for children and pregnant women? +
Homoeopathic remedies are highly diluted and gentle, making them safe for children, infants, and pregnant women when prescribed by a qualified practitioner. Dr. Shivhare has extensive experience treating paediatric cases and can advise appropriately during pregnancy.
How long does homoeopathic treatment typically take? +
Duration varies with the nature and chronicity of the condition. Acute illnesses can respond within days. Chronic conditions — such as long-standing skin disease, PCOD, or migraine — may require 3–12 months of consistent treatment. Improvement is usually noticeable within the first 4–8 weeks.
Can I take homoeopathy alongside my existing medications? +
In most cases, yes. Homoeopathic remedies are non-toxic and do not chemically interact with conventional medicines. Dr. Shivhare recommends informing her of all medications you take so she can consider the complete clinical picture and advise accordingly.
What are the clinic's consultation hours? +
The clinic operates Monday to Saturday: 9:30 AM to 3:00 PM in the morning session, and 5:30 PM to 9:00 PM in the evening session. On-call visits can be arranged separately by calling the clinic.
Where is Shivhare's Clinic located? +
The clinic is located at 52, South Ambazari Road, Subhash Nagar, Trimurti Nagar, Nagpur — opposite Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose High School. It is easily accessible by auto and private transport.
Do you offer on-call or home-visit consultations? +
Yes. Dr. Shivhare offers on-call visits for patients who are unable to visit the clinic in person. Please call the clinic to check availability and arrange a suitable time for a home visit within Nagpur.
